The impressions, clicks, and position figures above are exact values from the
Search Console API, not estimates. Do not round them into new claims or
extrapolate a trend from a single snapshot.

Do not speculate about causes the data does not show: no algorithm updates, no
competitor activity, no seasonality, unless the provided data itself contains
evidence for it.

Position is impression-weighted across queries, not a rank-tracker position.
Read it as a relative signal and as deltas between snapshots, never as "this
ranks number N".
